candealwithcomplexdynamicsofself-drivingvehicles.
Based on the extended cellar automata, we first model the car generation process

peakhoursandnonpeakhours. Thenwemodelthebehaviorsofvehicle-followingand

probabilitymodeltoanalyzethebehavioroftrafficflowathighwayintersections.
Weverifythefeasibilityofourmodelbyrunningvarioussimulations. Ourmodelis

volumes. We find that self-driving cars can stabilize the performance of traffic system

Then we apply our model to the data of four highways in the Greater Seattle area.
Ourresultsshowthatthetrafficcapacityincreaseswithrisingpercentageofself-driving
