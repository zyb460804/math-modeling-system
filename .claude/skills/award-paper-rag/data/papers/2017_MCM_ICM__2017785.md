6.2 Weaknesses
Oneweaknessliesinourassumptionthatthesolefactordrivingfishmigrationisthechanging

aremyriadotherfactorsthatdeterminethesustainabilityofmigration(e.g.,therelativeabundance

Finally, the model’s lack of seasonal data makes it impractical to use the model to predict

on a specific day. Due to seasonal weather, the ocean water’s temperature oscillates significantly

the oscillations of the ocean temperature throughout the year. However, our model still accurately
